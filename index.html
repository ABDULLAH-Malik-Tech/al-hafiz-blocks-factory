About our factory

Built on Experience. Focused on Quality.

Established in 2018, Al Hafiz Blocks Factory is a concrete block manufacturing business based in Talagang, Punjab, Pakistan.

We work with construction customers, including house builders, contractors, masons and building-material dealers. Our goal is to provide clear communication, fair dealing and dependable service.

  • Machine-made concrete block production
  • Attention to material management and wastage
  • Focus on dimensions and proper curing
  • Customer enquiries and bulk order discussions
